; DESERT EAST 8 - Phase 13, where the ground has mostly gone
; (92x68). The Astral Sea is the map; what is left of the worlds
; is islands in it, joined by causeways two tiles wide. Every walk
; across is a ledge with the Sea on both sides.
; Two islands have no causeway at all: drawn, lit, weathered, and
; impossible to stand on. A collision this big should have places
; in it that Chuck can only look at.
; The causeways change material halfway, because nobody built
; them -- they are what happens to be left of the ground between
; two things that were never near each other.
; East is the trio, and the end of the walk.
