; DESERT EAST 7 - Phase 13, where the collision stops being tidy
; (88x64). Nine pieces of world and no majority: two of Chult in
; opposite corners, two of desert that never touch, and a ship's
; deck against a snowfield. Every border between them is torn.
; The way through is guaranteed rather than found: a corridor is
; walked west to east before anything is torn and protected after,
; and it is made of whatever world it happens to be crossing.
; Six kinds of enemy, each standing in the world it belongs to.
; East leads on to the eighth, where the ground has mostly gone.
