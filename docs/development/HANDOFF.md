## Implementation

- `F` maps to the named `scratch` action. Scratch lasts 0.18 seconds,
  pauses movement, reaches a short distance in Chuck's facing direction,
  and draws two pale one-pixel wind streaks. There is no elaborate combo,
  equipment, stat, or progression layer.
- `SewerRat` is a reusable entity with a generated two-frame 10x8 sprite,
  an 8x6 footprint, light contact damage, and one-hit defeat behavior.
  It is visibly smaller than Chuck's 12x14 sprite.
- Three `q` map markers spawn rats on dirt beyond the Astral gap. The map
  narrows to one tile for three rows, and living rat bodies reject Chuck's
  movement, so the group must be scratched to continue.
- `scratch_first_target()` resolves at most one living target per swipe,
  preserving the explicit one-hit/one-rat rule.
- "Press F to scratch" appears only near the post-gap choke while rats
  remain. Jump and interaction hint behavior is unchanged.
- `touches_astral_fall_zone()` keeps hazard detection separate from scene
  choreography. `SanitySystem.deplete()` explicitly bypasses damage i-frames
  for lethal traversal failures.
- Scratch rendering derives its lead/trail positions from attack progress,
  producing visible motion across the existing 0.18-second window without
  changing hit reach or timing. The SFX is generated by the existing pure-
  stdlib audio tool and played once from `WorldScene` when a swipe begins.
